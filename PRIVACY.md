# Privacy policy for nMIDI Editor

*Last updated: 14 November 2026*

nMIDI Editor does not collect, store or send any personal information.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything nMIDI Editor stores stays on your computer:

- **Settings** (theme, window layout, MIDI ports, editor options) and the **list of recently opened files**,
  in `%AppData%\nMIDIEditor`.
- **Autosave copies** of the song you are editing, in its `autosave` subfolder, so work can be recovered after a crash.
- **MIDI files** only where you choose to save them.

You can delete this data at any time by deleting the `%AppData%\nMIDIEditor` folder or uninstalling the app.

## Network and devices

nMIDI Editor does not connect to the internet. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. It uses your
MIDI devices only to play songs and to record from a MIDI keyboard, and only while the app is open.

## Microsoft Store

If you install the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect diagnostic data such as crash reports
under its own [privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). The developer only sees
anonymous totals and crash reports in Partner Center.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.
